PRE — STRETCHES
Seven numbered blocks. Left-Lead on every unilateral hold. Ease — do not force.
☐  1. Spine Circles
Sets: 3 | Reps: 6–8 / way | Hold: 1 sec | Rest: 20 sec
3 Lead Unique Rule: 3-second circle each way. Hands under shoulders. Knees under hips.
Do this properly:
Quadruped. Draw a slow circle with the whole spine — tailbone, ribs, then chest.
Reverse the circle. Keep the neck long.
Use a strict 3-Second Negative on every lowering / return phase.
Avoid this:
Collapsing into the lumbar spine.
